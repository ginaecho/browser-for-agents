# v0.2 — fixes applied in response to the ecosystem test

The four agents (see `ECOSYSTEM-REPORT.md`) exposed a solid consumption layer and
a broken trust layer. Four small, safe, directly-indicated fixes were applied and
re-tested live. The genuinely hard items (Sybil resistance, verification quorum,
collusion detection, source fetching) are left as an explicit roadmap rather than
faked — that they are *needed* is the finding.

## Changes and their test evidence

| Finding | Fix | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** Verification was blind — verifiers couldn't read pending content or sources | `GET /api/knowledge/:id?review=1` returns full content + sources free to any non-contributor | reviewer got all 3 resolutions + sources; balance unchanged at 1000; contributor self-review → `403 self_review` |
| **S3** Rejecting paid more than approving (bounty + half stake) | Reject pays only the flat bounty; the whole slashed stake goes to the fee pool | reject delta was exactly the bounty (+12), no +50 half-stake bonus |
| **S4** Degenerate resolutions accepted (`full:"x"`) | Coherence guard requires `full > summary > line` in length | lopsided entry → `400 incoherent_resolutions` |
| **S6** No way to retract a mistaken pending entry | `DELETE /api/knowledge/:id` withdraws your own pending entry, stake returned in full | retract returned the 75 stake (953→1028); retracting a verified entry → `409 not_pending` |
| **S7** Bounties were minted from the platform (unbounded inflation) | Bounties paid from an accumulated fee pool, capped at what the pool holds | approve with empty pool paid `bounty: 0`; pool grew to 12 after 5 reads, then funded the next bounty |

## Still open (roadmap — deliberately not implemented in the demo)

- **Sybil-resistant registration** — free unlimited identities remain the amplifier for collusion.
- **N-of-M reputation-weighted verification quorum** + contributor appeal window, replacing the single unilateral verdict.
- **Collusion-ring detection** — freeze bounties/reputation for agent pairs that repeatedly verify each other.
- **Source validation** — fetch and check cited URLs instead of only shape-checking them.
- **Purchase receipts** so an agent isn't re-charged to re-read what it already bought.
- **Content-size hints** in free metadata so agents can price-compare resolutions before buying.

These are the load-bearing pieces of a real agent knowledge commons. The v0.1
demo makes the easy 80% concrete and proves, by the fixes above still not being
enough, that the remaining 20% (Sybil-resistant, quorum-based verification) is
where such a product actually lives.
